# Pipeline review — which results are OK, which are not, and why

Machine-readable version: **`pipeline_review.csv`** (open in Excel). One row per
arm/tag, sorted by tier, with a `location` column saying which folder each
pipeline lives in. Generated by `build_pipeline_review.py` from the live
`results.csv` numbers + the verdicts below. Canonical protocol throughout:
`forward / with_aobt / keep_all`.

> **Tier E is now quarantined on disk.** The six over-tuned arms have been moved
> to **`overtuned/`** and removed from `config.ARMS`, so `run_all.py` no longer
> builds them and `compare.py` no longer ranks them. Their code stays importable
> (`config.OVERTUNED_DIR` is on `sys.path`) because the runs under
> `artifacts/runs/` are the evidence for the verdict. See
> [`overtuned/README.md`](overtuned/README.md). `results.csv` is unchanged - it
> harvests `artifacts/runs/`, and the runs happened.
>
> Two tier-E arms were deliberately **left in place**, because "over-tuned" is
> not why they are tier E: `per_airport_mlp` is non-functional, and
> `per_airport_antigravity` is a byte-identical duplicate of `per_airport`.
> The `log1p` rows are *tags* inside the tier-A `pipeline_lightgbm/` and
> `pipeline_per_airport/` folders, not pipelines of their own.

## How the tiers are decided

The metric that decides this is **pooled RMSE**, but ~3 unlearnable date-artifact
rows carry ~52 % of pooled test squared error, so pooled test RMSE is a lottery.
Every verdict is read against the **in-band RMSE** (y ≤ 7200 s) companion and
against the **seed spread** — the build's own rule is that *a win margin smaller
than the arm's seed-to-seed swing is not a real ranking*.

| tier | meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | trust it, build on it |
| **B** | real but subsumed by a tier-A arm — keep for the ablation, don't ship |
| **C** | clean negative — measured, no signal; keep the record, don't use |
| **D** | usable only with a caveat (blend ingredient / val-fitted / single seed) |
| **E** | over-tuned or non-functional — do not trust the number, do not ship |
| **P** | pending — not enough runs to judge |

## Tier E — NOT OK (over-tuned / do not trust)

### The `airport_*_multimodel` ladder — ranking is seed noise

`airport_unsup_multimodel`, `airport_wtc_rec_multimodel`, `airport_wtc_multimodel`
and `airport_runway_multimodel` are **one arm** — the same per-airport tree×MLP
blend as `multimodel_antigravity`, with the blend weight bucketed by
wake-category / runway / record-status / k-means cluster. They were ranked into a
"champion" ladder on seeds 42/43/44.

**`airport_operator_multimodel` has been reclassified E → A and is NOT
quarantined.** Re-reading its `train.py` against tier-A `multimodel_antigravity`:
identical structure — the same 10 per-airport alphas, the same least-squares
solve, the same `forward_inner` surface, **no bucketing axis and no extra
hyperparameter**. Only the tree-side parent differs. Losing a rank to a sibling at
one seed makes it *unrankable*, which is a different finding from over-tuned. It
is the last honest member of the family, and over 6 seeds it is *more stable* than
`multimodel_antigravity` (val SD 1.88 vs 5.17) because leaning on
`airport_operator_blend` absorbs a bad MLP draw.

**Complete 6-seed re-run (`tests_seed_robustness/result_seeds_42-47.txt`):**

| metric | #1→#2 margin | that arm's seed SD | margin ÷ noise | family spread | one member's own seed range |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| val | 0.40 s | 1.96 s | **0.20** | 2.62 s | 4.41 s |
| test | 0.24 s | 4.74 s | **0.05** | 4.54 s | 13.11 s |
| val in-band | 0.01 s | 1.50 s | **0.01** | 3.03 s | 4.37 s |
| test in-band | 0.23 s | 0.59 s | **0.40** | 1.53 s | 1.00 s |

Worth stating precisely, since the seeds 42–46 reading in an earlier draft of
this file was harsher than the finished data supports: with seed 47 in,
`airport_unsup_multimodel` *does* hold #1 on the mean of all four metrics, and the
per-seed ordering is fairly stable. **That does not rescue the ladder** — the
margins are 0.01–0.40 s against seed SDs of 0.6–4.7 s, and **the entire six-arm
family is separated by less than one member's own seed range**. The build's own
rule (a win margin smaller than the arm's seed swing is not a real ranking)
disqualifies every promotion in it.

What the ladder pays for that: fitted blend cells go **10 → ~240**, and
`min_cell_rows` was walked **1000 → 300 → 30** down the ladder, each step lowering
the bar so the next, finer bucketing could still be fit. `airport_unsup_multimodel`
adds k-means K=6 × 4 regimes × λ=2000 shrinkage — three hyperparameters with no
recorded sweep — and its `KMeans` is hard-coded to `random_state=42`, so the
clustering does **not** vary with `--seed` and the sweep above *understates* its
true variance.

**The blend under them is fine — it's `multimodel_antigravity` (tier A). The
bucketing refinements on top are the over-tuning.**

### Other tier-E

| arm | why NOT OK |
|---|---|
| **airport_debiased_multimodel** | **The decisive case** — worse than a ranking problem. Single seed (42). `coef_mid=[0,0,1]` hard-coded rather than fitted; `global_alpha=0.720`, `gamma_pos=0.50`, `gamma_neg=0.10`, slope gates at `±5.0`, thresholds `18000`/`50000` are magic constants. Its **own docstring justifies them by the score they produce**: *"Lowering threshold_mid to 18,000.0 s … dropping Val RMSE from 238.88 s to 238.53 s"* and *"gamma=0.50 … dropping Test RMSE to 226.83 s"*. A knob whose value is chosen by the test number is fitting the evaluation surface, not the data. Plus a per-airport winter `doy_cos` slope fitted on `forward_inner` and applied to the Nov–Dec **test** rows. Never adjudicated by `compare.py`; `stage11_segment_audit.py` independently found its `>50,000 s` band is 20 rows/year at residual std **13,488 s**. |
| airport_multiseed_multimodel | The seed-averaging is legitimate; the base (`airport_unsup_multimodel`) is not. |
| per_airport_mlp | Broke — LIRF val RMSE 1128 s from tail scarcity. Not over-tuned, non-functional. |
| per_airport_antigravity | Byte-identical to `per_airport`. Drop it. |
| `lightgbm log1p` · `per_airport log1p` | The log-target experiment run for this review. 3-seed means: val in-band better (−16 / −7 s) but **test in-band flat-to-worse** and pooled RMSE **+32 / +39 s**, LB-weighted **+27 / +32 s**. The tail decides the metric and log1p de-weights the tail. Rejected — details in `tests_log_target/RESULT.md`. |

## Tier D — caveat only

- **mlp_antigravity** — standalone test RMSE swings 65–81 s across seeds. Safe only *inside* the blend. Never select on it alone.
- **ensemble_antigravity** — NNLS weights fitted on **val labels**; its val number is in-sample. Test seed range 46 s.
- **cross_blend** untagged / `w_per_airport` / `w_global` / `w_inner_tuned` — val-fitted, degenerate, or single-seed weight modes. Use `w_inner`.
- **per_airport_stack** (+ `inner2` / `inner3` / `inner2_tuned`) — beaten by `per_airport` alone at 9/10 airports; the carried variants are single-seed.

## Tier C — clean negatives (correctly rejected, keep the record)

`lightgbm_recency` (recency weighting — 0.3 s vs a 1.7 s spread), `lightgbm_unsup`
(val −1.4 s / test +3.1 s), `lightgbm_arrfeat`, `per_airport_flighttype` (LOST),
`per_airport_tuned` (per-airport HP buys 0.12 s over one global config),
`tri_blend` (LOST — one global weight can't express the per-airport tree/neural
mix), `feature_pruned_antigravity`, `delay_twostage_antigravity` (LOST),
`xgboost_antigravity` (diversity only), and the `lightgbm lrXX_*` capacity-tuning
sweep (buys 6.5 s val, LightGBM-only so it can't re-rank against untuned rivals).

## `airport_lirf_identity_multimodel` — resolved out of tier P

It has runs now, and it splits: the `idband` tag (tier **A**) applies only the
verified 25.2–50 k telemetry identity — zero fitted parameters, `y − delta =
0.0 ± 2.9 s` over the full year's 34 rows, in-band RMSE exactly unchanged. The
untagged run (tier **B**) also bets on the `>50 k` band, which
`stage11_segment_audit.py` measures at 20 rows/year and residual std 13,488 s —
a declared tail bet, not a finding. This is the *right* correction to
`airport_debiased`'s hand-fit.

**One consequence of the quarantine to fix:** its `BASE_ARM` is
`airport_multiseed_multimodel` → `airport_unsup_multimodel`, both now in
`overtuned/`. Its own override is clean, but **its headline number inherits the
base's optimism**. Re-point it at `airport_operator_multimodel` (or
`multimodel_antigravity`) to read it as a clean tier-A add-on. It also had no
`pipeline_catalog.csv` row, so it was silently missing from
`pipelines_overview.csv`; that row has been added.

## Is there headroom left? (the forward-looking read)

The tiers above say whether a result is *trustworthy*. That is a different
question from whether the arm has anything left to give. Per tier:

| tier | arms | headroom |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | `airport_operator_multimodel` | **Open** — never refit on the full 12-month period. That is the one live candidate (plan 12). |
| **A** | `multimodel_antigravity`, `cross_blend w_inner` | **Closed** — the same lever as the winner. Keep one for the ablation; folding both in adds nothing. |
| **A** | `per_airport`, `airport_operator_blend` | **Closed as standalone** — they are the winner's parents; improving them improves it, which is what the refit does. |
| **A** | `lightgbm`, `ridge`, `naive_offblock` | **Closed by design** — floors and sanity anchors, not contenders. Their value is that they do not move. |
| **B** | `airport_lirf_identity_multimodel idband` | **Open, small and free** — a zero-parameter verified identity. Needs re-pointing off the quarantined base (plan 12, candidate 2). |
| **B** | `per_operator`, `per_airport_arrfeat` | **Closed** — subsumed by `airport_operator_blend` / inside seed noise. |
| **C** | all nine | **Closed — these are the measurements.** Each one already answered its question with a negative. Re-running them with different settings is how the `overtuned/` ladder happened. |
| **D** | `mlp_antigravity` | **Open** — it is the only high-variance component left. Val RMSE SD over the seeds on disk: `per_airport` **0.27**, `airport_operator_blend` **0.80**, `mlp_antigravity` **3.52** — and the blend built on them, `airport_operator_multimodel`, sits at **2.07**. The tree side is nearly deterministic, so essentially all of the winner's seed variance is the MLP. Variance is the target here, not accuracy. |
| **D** | `ensemble_antigravity`, `cross_blend` (untagged), `per_airport_stack` | **Closed** — val-fitted or beaten by a parent. The honest versions already exist. |
| **E** | the six in `overtuned/` | **Closed and quarantined.** |

### Should we tune hyperparameters? Almost certainly not.

This build has already measured that lever twice, and both answers are in the
repo:

| lever | measured gain (val RMSE) |
|---|---|
| `stage7_tune_lightgbm.py` — full capacity sweep (lr × leaves × depth) | **6.5 s** |
| `per_airport_tuned` — a config chosen *per airport*, 10 separate val selections | **0.12 s** |
| *(for scale)* the outlier-policy fix — one constant, `drop_7200` → `keep_all` | **27 s** |

One constant changed by reasoning about the loss surface beat an exhaustive
capacity sweep by 4×, and per-airport tuning bought nothing at all for ten bites
at the validation apple. **Tuning is the lowest-yield lever measured on this
build.** The gains here have come from structure (segmenting by airport, blending
tree × neural) and from getting the *protocol* right (outlier policy, honest
weight surfaces) — not from hyperparameters.

The one exception is already handled: `lightgbm lr05_lv511_md50` is kept solely
to freeze a config for the submission refit, which is what tier C's
"scope-limited" note means.

## Bottom line — what to ship

**`airport_operator_multimodel`**, refit on the **full** 12-month period, with
the MLP seed-averaged (`--mlp-seeds 42,43,44`) and
`airport_lirf_identity_multimodel --band idband` as a free add-on on top. Same
10-parameter per-airport blend as `multimodel_antigravity` and
`cross_blend w_inner` (all three are the same lever and are a statistical tie),
but the steadiest of them across seeds.

Three changes were made under a **pre-registered** acceptance rule
([plan 12](plans/12_plan_full_refit_and_improvement.md)) — written before any run,
so the decision could not be made after seeing the number:

| change | status | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Full-period refit** (Jan–Dec instead of Jan–Aug) | done | pool +50.3 %; blend weights byte-identical, alpha and feature fingerprint unchanged. **No accuracy claim** — `full` has no holdout, so it is adopted on the principle, not a measurement. |
| **LIRF identity override re-pointed** off the quarantined base, `idband` only | **adopted** | 3/3 seeds improve on val *and* test; in-band **identical to the decimal** at every seed, as the mechanism requires (it touches 5 rows above 25,200 s). |
| **MLP seed-averaged inside the blend** | **adopted** | 3/3 seeds improve on all four metrics; mean val −2.38 s vs the incumbent's 2.07 s SD. The real result: **seed spread 4.090 s → 0.020 s.** |

Note what none of these are: no new bucketing axis, no hyperparameter sweep, no
constant chosen by the score it produced. Two are *more data* and *less variance*;
the third is a verified zero-parameter identity.

Everything that sits above it in `results.csv` — `airport_unsup`,
`airport_wtc_rec`, `airport_multiseed`, `airport_debiased` — is that same blend
with over-tuned trimmings and is now in `overtuned/`. Do not select on it.
